Data sources

  • Crater terrain: CTX stereo elevation model of Jezero, 20 m/px, made for Mars 2020 Terrain-Relative Navigation (JEZ_ctx_B_soc_008_DTM, USGS Astrogeology / NASA-JPL), resampled to 60 m. Routes are planned on this.
  • Crater imagery: CTX orthomosaic, 6 m/px (USGS / NASA-JPL / MSSS). Grayscale source with a display tint.
  • Surrounding 240 km: Mars HRSC-MOLA Blended DEM v2, 200 m/px (NASA MGS MOLA + ESA Mars Express HRSC; USGS Astrogeology) and the Viking MDIM2.1 mosaic via NASA Mars Trek. Mars curvature is applied beyond the crater zone.
  • Rover traverse: Perseverance drive path and end-of-drive localizations, sols 0–2022, NASA/JPL-Caltech Mars 2020 MMGIS.
  • Space weather: NASA CCMC DONKI (ccmc.gsfc.nasa.gov/DONKI): CMEs whose WSA-ENLIL model runs predicted a Mars impact, Jan 2024 – Nov 2026. Live refresh through api.nasa.gov.
  • Rover images: Perseverance raw images, sols 0–4, NASA/JPL-Caltech.
  • 3D models: Astronaut and Mars 2020 Perseverance rover from NASA 3D Resources (github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources), shown at true scale (1.85 m astronaut, 2.2 m rover). Zoom in to see how small they are against the 60 m terrain grid. The rover's driven tracks mark ground proven traversable, and each plan reports how much of the walk follows them.
  • Mars time: Mars24 (Allison & McEwen 2000, NASA GISS). Radiation baseline: MSL RAD, about 0.64 mSv/day (Hassler et al. 2014).

Method

  • Sense: slope from the elevation model; ground steeper than the limit is no-go.
  • Decide: A* search for the lowest-energy path using Minetti et al. (2002) walking cost against gradient; outbound and return planned separately.
  • Act: go / caution / no-go. The walk plus science time must fit the suit limit with a 20% reserve, the crew must be rested, and if a storm arrives mid-walk the crew must reach shelter in time.
  • Baseline: every route is compared with the straight line (dashed).

Limits

  • A 60 m grid can't see boulders; a real traverse needs HiRISE 1 m terrain.
  • Walking speed (Tobler × 0.7 for the suit) and energy are Earth-derived planning estimates.
  • CME arrival times are uncertain by hours, so alerts are conservative. The crew check is a screening flag, not a medical assessment.
